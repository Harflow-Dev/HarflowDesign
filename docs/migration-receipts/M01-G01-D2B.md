## Recommended owner decisions

1. Show qualified observed totals only. Unknown/loss/summary-limited totals remain unavailable, not zero or invented lower bounds. Keep independently sound summary and snapshot facts when only the activity window is full. Qualified zero requires genuinely tool-free qualified captured history.
2. Use an Original request disclosure with separate complete-inline, verified, preview, unverified, truncated, integrity-failed, unavailable and loading meanings. Copy labels reflect exactly what is known; read more replaces a bounded prefix and never promises unlimited/private-file access.
3. Keep capture health inline and independent of execution status. Retry observations without restarting a task; retain loss through retries/rebases and require explicit replacement of a reset/full reading window.
4. Preserve first-visible occurrence and relative offset on prepend/resize, with truthful edge/clamp handling. Scope identities by source, observation/window lifetime and occurrence, never global sequence order; separate consumer rebase from actual source change.
5. Keep normalized public records/stubs and recovered transcript material separately labelled in the retained Events destination. No original-JSONL/private-path claim or recovered-data counting authority is inferred.

These are recommendations for explicit owner review, not approvals inferred from this receipt, tests or a local commit. The tracker owns any governing ledger changes; the proposed entry is in the semantic packet and the dirty ledger is untouched.
