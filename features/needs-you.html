Show every pending input, whoever started the run.

A Needs you entry gathers approvals, questions and recovery checkpoints from tasks and workflow runs, including nested child workflows and nodes. Harflow answers only when the engine reports that the caller has ended. These are deterministic fixtures; no engine is contacted and nothing is sent.

Try the interaction

These controls stand in for the engine and sit outside the app surface. Send an answer, then choose the engine’s response. On the waiting state, deliver the caller ending to watch the request become answerable.

Fixture controls are enabled when the matching state is reached.

Behavior under review

Needs you lists every pending input the app can read, whoever started the run. Only requests whose caller the engine confirms ended, or that Harflow already owns, can be answered here. A request whose caller is connected is shown read-only and says who it is waiting for. Unknown caller identity fails closed. Answering hands the run to Harflow; the original caller can no longer answer it, and the engine may still refuse on submit.

No opt-in for answering while the caller is connected exists in the engine, so none is drawn. Browser controls, materials, the notification banner and the menu-bar popover approximate native surfaces.
